BiomEdit has secured conditional USDA licensure for its engineered bacterial biologic targeting necrotic enteritis in poultry. The product reduced necrotic enteritis-associated mortality by 48.7% in a pivotal efficacy study, with commercial evaluations planned for over 35 million broilers.
