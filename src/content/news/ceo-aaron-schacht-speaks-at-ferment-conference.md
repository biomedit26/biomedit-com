Each spring, Ginkgo Bioworks holds its annual Ferment conference showcasing the most exciting new ideas in synthetic biology. This year, BiomEdit CEO Aaron Schacht was asked to participate in the "Application Lightning Round" and give a 5 minute presentation about how the company is using synthetic biology to solve some of the most intractable problems in animal health.
