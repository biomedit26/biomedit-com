The Indianapolis Business Journal reports on BiomEdit's USDA conditional licensure milestone, adding company-history context: BiomEdit spun out of Elanco Animal Health and Ginkgo Bioworks in 2022, relocated this summer from Greenfield to the Indiana Biosciences Research Institute at 16 Tech Innovation District, and currently employs about 30 people. The article also recaps BiomEdit's Series B and the newly initiated Series C.
