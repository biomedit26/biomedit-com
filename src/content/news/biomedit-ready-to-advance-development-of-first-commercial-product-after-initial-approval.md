INDIANAPOLIS - Indianapolis-based startup BiomEdit recently received conditional licensure for its first commercial product from the U.S. Department of Agriculture’s Center for Veterinary Biologics. The company is bringing science related to gut health and probiotics to the chicken coop, and it’s caught the attention of the nation’s poultry industry.
Necrotic enteritis is a disease most humans haven’t heard of, but chicken producers know it well. It’s triggered by a common bacteria in the bird’s gut that damages its intestines. Altogether, it costs the broiler industry up to $6 billion annually.
