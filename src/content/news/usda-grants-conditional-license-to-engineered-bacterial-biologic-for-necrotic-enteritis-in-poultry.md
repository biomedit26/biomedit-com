The US Department of Agriculture Center for Veterinary Biologics (USDA CVB) has granted a conditional license to an engineered bacterial biologic designed to reduce necrotic enteritis (NE)-associated mortality in broiler chickens. The company that developed it, BiomEdit, says it believes the product is the first genetically engineered probiotic biologic licensed for use in any animal species worldwide.
