**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

GREENFIELD, IND. (August 25, 2026) – BiomEdit, an animal health biotechnology company, today announced the acquisition of Folium Science's technology platform and pipeline assets, expanding its Programmable Veterinary Biologics™ capabilities with new CRISPR-based microbiome technology and a second commercial-stage poultry product.

The acquisition brings Folium Science's Guided Biotics® platform, a CRISPR-Cas-based technology that selectively targets specific bacterial populations within the microbiome, into BiomEdit's pipeline. It also adds BiomElix® One, a non-GMO feed additive approved by Brazil's Ministry of Agriculture and Livestock (MAPA) that targets Salmonella in the poultry gut microbiome, giving BiomEdit a second poultry product program alongside CPerf LLV (BE-101).

"This acquisition strengthens our platform with a proven, complementary technology and adds a second product to our pipeline that's already approved in a major market," said Aaron Schacht, CEO of BiomEdit. "Guided Biotics gives us a new tool for precisely targeting harmful bacteria in the microbiome, extending what we can do beyond strain engineering alone."

With the acquisition, BiomEdit's Programmable Veterinary Biologics™ platform now combines two complementary approaches: engineering bacterial strains to produce therapeutic payloads, and using CRISPR-based technology to selectively target bacteria already present in the microbiome. Together, these capabilities expand the range of animal health and production challenges BiomEdit can address.

**About [BiomEdit](http://www.biomedit.com/):** BiomEdit is an animal health biotechnology company leveraging the power of the microbiome and synthetic biology to develop next-generation solutions for livestock and pet health. Founded in 2022, BiomEdit is backed by leading investors including Anterra Capital, Viking Global, Nutreco, AgriZeroNZ, Elevate and Betagro Ventures among others.
